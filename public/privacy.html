Privacy and Static Site Notes

This is a static informational site. It does not run a shopping cart, user account system, comment form, newsletter signup, or private document upload. A typical visit may still create standard web-server or CDN logs, such as requested URL, time, browser type, referrer, and rough network information. Those logs are used for security, delivery, and basic troubleshooting rather than personal profiling.

External links may lead to product reviews, prior cloud articles, or public resources. Once a reader leaves this static site, the destination site controls its own privacy practices, cookies, analytics, and commerce flows. Review those policies before submitting personal or payment information elsewhere.

Images are stored as static assets and are used to support the article layout. The site does not ask readers to upload photos of their desks or paperwork. If a correction is requested, only public URLs and non-sensitive notes should be shared.

The editorial boundary is intentionally simple: explain vertical desktop file organizer selection, keep article links clear, and avoid collecting private information. If a future version adds forms or analytics, this page should be updated before those tools are enabled.

The Privacy page is intentionally plain because the site is static. Readers do not need accounts, cookies, uploads, or forms to use the guide. Server delivery may still create ordinary technical logs, but the article itself does not need personal details to function. External links are boundaries: once a reader follows a retailer, review, or cloud-chain link, that destination controls its own data practices and purchase flow.
